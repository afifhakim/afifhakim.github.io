This presentation explores the role of vegetation as a nature-based solution for preventing coastal erosion at Nipah-Nipah Beach, Penajam Paser Utara, near Indonesia's new capital city (IKN). Using Delft3D numerical modelling, the study evaluates the effects of fully vegetated, partially vegetated, and non-vegetated coastal conditions on accumulated erosion and sedimentation.

The results demonstrate that mangrove vegetation can substantially reduce erosion compared to non-vegetated conditions, highlighting the importance of integrating ecological approaches into sustainable coastal development.
