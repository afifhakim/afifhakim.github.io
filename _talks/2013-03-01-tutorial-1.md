As a trainer in the Geosoftware.ID Regular Course, I delivered training on hydrodynamic and morphodynamic modeling using Delft3D. The course introduced participants to numerical modelling concepts and practical applications of Delft3D for understanding flow dynamics, sediment transport, and coastal and riverine morphological processes.

Beyond formal training courses, Delft3D can also be learned through private mentoring and several educational platforms available on Instagram. These learning opportunities provide alternative pathways for students, researchers, and practitioners interested in developing their skills in hydroinformatics and numerical modelling.
