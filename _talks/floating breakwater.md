This presentation discusses the concept of floating breakwaters as a nature-conscious coastal protection solution for sheltered coastal environments. The talk highlights the potential application of floating structures to reduce wave energy, protect shorelines, and support sustainable coastal management. It was presented as part of the WINNER 2023 Poster Category Winner event.
