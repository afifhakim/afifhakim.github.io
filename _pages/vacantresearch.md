### Daftar Topik Penelitian Tugas Akhir (Skripsi) Tahun 2026-2027

Berikut merupakan beberapa topik penelitian yang tersedia bagi mahasiswa
Program Studi Teknik Sipil, Institut Teknologi Kalimantan.

Topik penelitian mencakup bidang **hidrologi, hidraulika, transportasi sedimen,
proses sungai dan pesisir, operasi waduk, serta pengelolaan sumber daya air**,
dengan peluang untuk menggunakan pemodelan numerik, GIS, maupun pengamatan
lapangan.

### Research Areas

The available topics cover several areas of Civil Engineering and Water
Resources Engineering:

- 🌊 **Estuarine and Coastal Modelling**
- 🏞️ **River Hydraulics and Morphodynamics**
- 🏔️ **Sediment Transport**
- 💧 **Reservoir Operation and Water Supply**
- 🌧️ **Hydrology and Drought**
- 🌱 **Nature-Based Solutions**
- 💻 **Numerical Modelling and GIS**

Students are encouraged to discuss the research topic, methodology,
available data, and expected outcomes with the supervisor before
starting the graduation project.
